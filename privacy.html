Rewind

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Rewind is an app that lets you keep a record of your festivals, clubs and parties, and share them with your friends. This page explains what data is collected, why, and what your rights are.

Who is responsible

Rewind is published by Fabrice Journel. For any question about your data: yk7txkyvg8@privaterelay.appleid.com.

Data we collect

  • Your account: email address, display name, handle (@).
  • Your profile: your bio, your city — which you enter yourself — your profile photo and banner.
  • Your content: the events you create (name, date, place, description, type), the photos you add, the people you tag, your likes and the events you mark as attended.
  • Your connections: your friend requests and friendships.

Rewind never reads your phone's address book, nor your GPS location. The city is information you type, not a measured location.

Why

Only to make the app work: create your account, keep your memories, and let you share them with your friends. Legal basis (GDPR): performance of the service you request by creating an account.

What we do not do

  • No advertising.
  • No selling or renting your data to third parties.
  • No advertising trackers, no analytics tools.

Where your data is stored

Your data (account, content, photos) is hosted by Supabase, our technical provider, acting as a processor and not using your data for its own purposes.

The default illustration images (shown when you haven't added your own photo yet) are loaded from Unsplash; when this happens, your IP address may be visible to Unsplash, as with any image displayed on the web.

How long

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, it is erased.

Your rights

  • Delete your account and all your data, directly in the app: Profile → menu → "Delete my account". Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Access, correct or object to the processing of your data: write to us at the address above.
  • You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Moderation

You can report content or block a person from within the app. Reports are reviewed and acted upon.

Children

Rewind is not intended for children under 15 without their parents' consent.

Changes

This policy may change. The "last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
